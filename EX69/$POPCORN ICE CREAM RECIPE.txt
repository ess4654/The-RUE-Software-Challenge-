$POPCORN ICE CREAM RECIPE

- Fill Blender with Popcorn
- Add 1 Cup Milk
- Fill Blender with Popcorn (again)
- Add 1 Cup Milk (again)
- Blend
- Add 1/4 cube of Butter
- Blend

- Strain blended liquid to remove hard kernels and large pieces
- Add 1 cup of sugar into liquid as you boil it
- Let liquid cool
- Add 1 cup powdered milk to liquid
- use a mixer to combine the mixture
- Freeze mixture

- Instant Ice Cream
