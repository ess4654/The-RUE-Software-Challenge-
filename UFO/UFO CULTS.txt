Jack Parsons explicitly stated that he communicated with and received instructions from what modern ufology calls Non-Human Intelligence (NHI). However, because he operated decades before the terms "NHI" or "UFO" entered popular culture, Parsons used the terminology of occultism, ceremonial magic, and extra-dimensional entities. He firmly believed that rocket science and magic were merely two different dimensions of the exact same hidden universal force.Parsons documented his interactions with these entities through his extensive magical diaries, letters to British occultist Aleister Crowley, and his self-published manifestos.

1. The Babalon Working & The "Liber 49" Channeling (1946)The most direct instance of Parsons claiming to receive information from an NHI occurred during the Babalon Working. This was a series of intense rituals conducted in early 1946 alongside his then-scribe, future Scientology founder L. Ron Hubbard.

The Voice: In March 1946, while alone in the Mojave Desert following a ritual, Parsons claimed a disembodied, intelligent voice began dictating information directly to him.

The Text: He transcribed this communication into a short text called "Liber 49" (also known as The Book of Babalon).

The Content: Parsons explicitly stated that this book was not written by his own mind, but was channeled directly from an intelligent, multidimensional goddess energy named Babalon. The text contained specific, cryptic instructions on how to manifest this entity into human form.
