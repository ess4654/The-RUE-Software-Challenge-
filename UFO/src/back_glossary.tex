\chapter{Glossary}
\markboth{Glossary}{Glossary}

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Where a term is contested --- and a great many of
them are --- I have said so rather than picking a side and pretending the argument is settled.

\glossletter{A}

\gloss{AARO}{All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office. The U.S.\ Department of Defense office established in 2022 as successor to the earlier UAP task forces, and the current official channel for military reports.}

\gloss{AATIP}{Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program. A U.S.\ Department of Defense effort,
funded at roughly \$22 million between 2007 and 2012, revealed publicly on 16 December 2017.}

\gloss{AAWSAP}{Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program. The contract predecessor to AATIP, which funded the Skinwalker Ranch investigation through Bigelow Aerospace.}

\gloss{Abductee (experiencer)}{A person reporting an abduction experience. This book uses both terms descriptively: the experience is real \emph{as an experience}, and that says nothing yet about its cause.}

\gloss{Abduction (alien)}{A reported experience of being taken, usually involuntarily, aboard a craft
or into the control of non-human entities. Discussed in Chapter~\ref{ch:abduction}. The reports are
real; the question at issue is what produces them.}

\gloss{Abiogenesis}{The origin of life from non-living chemistry. Not observed and not reproduced in a laboratory, and the largest single unknown in the Drake equation.}

\gloss{Ahnenerbe}{A research institute founded by Heinrich Himmler in 1935 to seek evidence of ancient
Germanic and ``Aryan'' presence. It produced no valid archaeology and some of its personnel committed
atrocities. See Section~\ref{sec:aryan}.}

\gloss{Alcubierre drive}{A 1994 solution in general relativity in which a craft is carried by a region of contracting and expanding spacetime rather than moving through it. Mathematically permitted, and requiring a negative-energy budget nobody knows how to obtain.}

\gloss{Ancient astronaut hypothesis}{The claim that non-human visitors influenced early human civilisation and were recorded as gods. Unfalsifiable as usually stated; treated in this book as a problem about broken dependency chains rather than as a fantasy.}

\gloss{Anecdotal evidence}{Testimony from individual experience, unaccompanied by measurement or
independent record. It is evidence --- it is simply the weakest class of it, and it does not become
stronger by accumulating.}

\gloss{Anunnaki}{A class of Mesopotamian deities, recast by Zecharia Sitchin as extraterrestrial visitors from a planet he called Nibiru. The recasting has no support in Sumerian scholarship.}

\gloss{Argument from ignorance}{The fallacy of treating the absence of an explanation as support for a
particular explanation. ``Unidentified'' means unidentified.}

\gloss{Ariel School}{The September 1994 incident at a school near Ruwa, Zimbabwe, in which sixty-two children were asked to draw what they had seen. Evidentially interesting because the drawings were collected before the accounts had time to converge.}

\gloss{ATFLIR}{Advanced Targeting Forward-Looking Infrared: the U.S.\ Navy pod that produced the FLIR1, Gimbal and GoFast footage. Its gimbal, zoom steps and rotating optics account for several of the effects those videos are famous for.}

\gloss{Atlantis}{An island civilisation described by Plato in the \emph{Timaeus} and \emph{Critias}
(c.\ 360 BC) as an illustrative antagonist. See Chapter~\ref{ch:atlantis}.}

\glossletter{B}

\gloss{Ball lightning}{A rare, briefly luminous atmospheric phenomenon reported as a glowing sphere
lasting seconds. Poorly understood and only sporadically reproduced in the laboratory, which makes it
both a genuine candidate explanation for some low-level sightings and a convenient one for any
sighting at all. See \textbf{Swamp gas} for the same problem in a different costume.}

\gloss{Bard, Erik}{X-ray physicist and principal investigator at Skinwalker Ranch, responsible for
its instrument coverage. See Section~\ref{sec:ufologist}.}

\gloss{Base rate}{The underlying frequency of an event in a population. Ignoring it --- the base rate
fallacy --- is the most common error in reasoning about rare events, including sightings.}

\gloss{Biosignature / technosignature}{Observable evidence of life, and observable evidence of technology, respectively. The distinction carries the weight: a biosignature is chemistry, a technosignature implies an engineer.}

\gloss{Birnes, William J.}{Publisher and editor of \emph{UFO Magazine}, presenter of
\emph{UFO Hunters}, co-author with Philip Corso of \emph{The Day After Roswell}.}

\gloss{Blue Book (Project)}{The U.S.\ Air Force's public UFO investigation, 1952--1969. It examined
12,618 reports and classified 701 as unidentified.}

\gloss{Burden of proof}{The obligation to supply support for a claim, which rests on the party making
it. See Section~\ref{sec:burdenproof}.}

\glossletter{C}

\gloss{Cash--Landrum}{The December 1980 Texas case in which Betty Cash, Vickie Landrum and her grandson developed medically documented symptoms after a close approach to a diamond-shaped object with a flame plume. Genuinely unexplained, and not established as nuclear-related.}

\gloss{Cattle mutilation}{Livestock deaths with apparently excised soft tissue, reported in waves since the 1970s. Repeatedly investigated; the pathology of ordinary scavenging accounts for the characteristic pattern.}

\gloss{Chain of custody}{The unbroken documented record of who held a piece of physical evidence, when, and under what conditions. Almost no ufological sample has one, which is why almost no ufological sample can be argued about productively.}

\gloss{Circular reporting}{The situation in which a single claim is repeated by multiple parties who
each take the others' repetition as independent corroboration. AARO has attributed much of the
secret-UAP-programme literature to this mechanism. It is the reason volume of testimony is not the same
as weight of evidence. See Section~\ref{sec:immaculate}.}

\gloss{Close encounter}{A sighting at short range, classified by J.\ Allen Hynek into kinds: CE-1
(observed at close range), CE-2 (with physical effects), CE-3 (with occupants visible).}

\gloss{Coast to Coast AM}{The overnight American talk programme, hosted by Art Bell from 1988. Treated
in Chapter~\ref{ch:media} as the field's central artefact: the largest continuous archive of
first-person anomalous testimony ever assembled, gathered by a man whose entire method was to let
people finish their sentences.}

\gloss{COMETA report}{The 1999 French private study, written by senior defence and scientific figures, which concluded that the extraterrestrial hypothesis was the least implausible explanation remaining. Frequently described as a government document; it is not one.}

\gloss{Confabulation}{The production of detailed, confidently held false recollection with no intent to deceive. The single most important word in the abduction literature.}

\gloss{Confirmation bias}{The tendency to seek, notice, and remember information consistent with what
one already believes. It afflicts sceptics and believers identically. See Section~\ref{sec:confirmationbias}.}

\gloss{Correlation}{A statistical relationship between two variables. It does not establish that
either causes the other. See Section~\ref{sec:correlationvscausation}.}

\gloss{Crop circle}{A geometric formation of flattened standing crop. Human authorship was demonstrated
in 1991 by Doug Bower and Dave Chorley; the formations went on being made afterwards, which is the
most informative fact about them.}

\gloss{Crustal displacement}{The hypothesis, proposed by Charles Hapgood in 1958, that the Earth's
lithosphere can slip catastrophically over the mantle. Not supported; see Section~\ref{sec:sciencepoleshifting}.}

\gloss{Cryptid}{An animal whose existence is claimed but not established by specimen, type
description, or documented remains.}

\gloss{Cryptozoology}{The search for animals not recognised by zoology. Its method is this field's method, and its difficulty is the same one: eyewitness volume without a specimen.}

\gloss{CUFOS}{Center for UFO Studies. Founded by J.\ Allen Hynek in 1973, after Blue Book closed, to give reports a scientific home outside the Air Force.}

\gloss{Cult}{A group organised around a leader with claimed privileged access to information that
cannot be independently checked, typically exhibiting a dated prophecy, isolation from outside
contact, escalating financial and personal commitment, reinterpretation rather than abandonment of
failed predictions, and a doctrine treating the body or the world as something to be escaped. The
term describes a structure, not a set of beliefs: the structure is what does the harm. See
Section~\ref{sec:ufocults}.}

\glossletter{D}

\gloss{Disclosure}{In UFO discourse, the anticipated official admission of non-human intelligence. In
practice what has occurred is narrower: institutional acknowledgement of unexplained sensor data.}

\gloss{Drake equation}{A 1961 framework by Frank Drake for estimating the number of communicating
civilisations in the galaxy. Its value is pedagogical: it organises our ignorance into named
variables. See Chapter~\ref{ch:life}.}

\gloss{Drake, Frank}{Radio astronomer (1930--2022) who conducted the first modern SETI search in
1960 and formulated the Drake equation. Not a Ufologist, and a useful contrast with the field's
methods.}

\gloss{Dulce Base}{An alleged joint human and non-human underground facility beneath Archuleta Mesa, New Mexico, originating with Paul Bennewitz in the 1980s. No physical evidence has ever been produced.}

\gloss{Dunning--Kruger effect}{The observed pattern in which the least competent are the most confident. Cited in this book about the field, its critics, and its author.}

\glossletter{E}

\gloss{EBE}{Extraterrestrial Biological Entity. A term of art from U.S.\ documents and folklore for a
non-human organism of off-world origin.}

\gloss{Element 115 (moscovium)}{Bob Lazar's claimed fuel element, first synthesised in 2003 and named moscovium in 2016. The real isotope survives for milliseconds and cannot be a stable fuel, which makes it the field's most instructive near-miss.}

\gloss{ESP}{Extrasensory perception. An umbrella term covering telepathy, clairvoyance and
precognition. See Section~\ref{sec:parapsych}.}

\gloss{Estes Method}{A ghost-hunting procedure in which one participant is blindfolded and hearing only noise while the others ask questions. Accidentally a single-blind protocol, which is why it is the one technique in the genre worth arguing about.}

\gloss{Exoplanet}{A planet orbiting a star other than the Sun. Roughly six thousand are now confirmed, which converts one term of the Drake equation from a guess into a measurement.}

\gloss{Exotic matter}{Hypothetical material with negative energy density, required by most
faster-than-light and traversable-wormhole solutions. No such matter has been observed in bulk.}

\glossletter{F}

\gloss{Falcon Lake}{The May 1967 case of Stefan Michalak in Manitoba, who suffered a documented grid pattern of burns to the chest after an alleged close encounter. A real physical injury of unknown origin.}

\gloss{False memory}{A recollection of an event that did not happen, or happened differently, held with the same confidence as a true one. Demonstrated experimentally, not merely inferred.}

\gloss{Falsifiability}{The property of a claim that some possible observation could show it false. A
claim that no observation could contradict is not a scientific claim, however true it may be.}

\gloss{Fermi paradox}{The tension between the apparent likelihood of extraterrestrial civilisations
and the absence of any evidence of them.}

\gloss{FLIR}{Forward-Looking Infrared. A thermal imaging system that renders differences in apparent
infrared radiance, not visible light. It measures heat contrast against background, so it establishes
neither an object's size nor its distance, and its optics can generate glare and rotational artefacts
that resemble structured motion. See Section~\ref{sec:equipment}.}

\gloss{FOIA}{Freedom of Information Act. The U.S.\ statute under which most of the government UFO record cited in this book was released. A FOIA release proves a document exists; it does not certify that the document is true.}

\gloss{Foo fighter}{A term used by Allied aircrews in the Second World War for luminous objects
observed accompanying aircraft. See Section~\ref{sec:foofighters}.}

\gloss{Fravor, David}{U.S.\ Navy commander who reported the 2004 \emph{Nimitz} ``Tic Tac'' encounter.
See Section~\ref{sec:uap}.}

\gloss{Friedman, Stanton}{Nuclear physicist (1934--2019) and the first full-time professional
investigator of Roswell. The field's most effective advocate, and a case study in reaching a
conclusion too early. See Chapter~\ref{ch:roswell}.}

\gloss{Fugal, Brandon}{Utah commercial property executive (b.~1973) who bought Skinwalker Ranch in
2016 and produced the television series investigating it. Funder, landowner and beneficiary of the
same research at once, which is the reason its findings remain unverified rather than wrong. See
Sections~\ref{sec:ufologist} and~\ref{sec:skinwalker}.}

\glossletter{G}

\gloss{Ganzfeld}{An experimental protocol in parapsychology using uniform sensory input to reduce
noise, historically the most-cited body of Psi evidence and the subject of long dispute over
methodology and replication.}

\gloss{Geiger counter}{A gas-filled detector that registers individual ionising events, giving a count
rate rather than a dose or an isotope identification. Useful in the field only against a properly
established local background, since natural variation from geology and altitude routinely exceeds the
anomalies claimed for landing sites. See Section~\ref{sec:equipment}.}

\gloss{Gimbal (video)}{The 2015 U.S.\ Navy infrared clip showing an object that appears to rotate about its axis. Named for the sensor's own gimbal, which is also the leading explanation for the rotation.}

\gloss{GoFast (video)}{The 2015 U.S.\ Navy clip of an object apparently skimming the sea surface. The sensor's own range and angle data place it at altitude and at unremarkable speed.}

\gloss{Grandfather paradox}{The self-consistency problem raised by backwards time travel: preventing
the conditions of one's own journey. See Chapter~\ref{ch:time}.}

\gloss{Great Filter}{The proposed step on the path from chemistry to a communicating civilisation that almost nothing gets past. The most economical reading of the Fermi paradox, and so far untestable.}

\gloss{Greer, Steven}{Former emergency physician, founder of CSETI and organiser of the 2001
Disclosure Project; originator of the CE-5 protocol.}

\gloss{Grey}{The dominant modern description of a reported entity: small, hairless, grey-skinned, with
large black eyes. Its cultural saturation dates from the late 1980s, which matters when evaluating
testimony that describes it.}

\gloss{Groom Lake}{The dry lakebed in the Nevada Test and Training Range that is Area~51. The United States denied its existence for decades, which is the reason official denials about the adjacent Papoose Lake (S-4) carry so little evidential weight.}

\gloss{Ground-penetrating radar (GPR)}{A geophysical method that transmits radar pulses into the
ground and times reflections from boundaries where electrical properties change. It maps buried
disturbance, not buried objects, and its returns require excavation or independent data to interpret.
See Section~\ref{sec:equipment}.}

\glossletter{H}

\gloss{Hessdalen}{A valley in Norway with recurring luminous phenomena, monitored by an automated station since 1998. The nearest thing this field has to a repeatable observable.}

\gloss{Hollow Earth}{The claim that the planet's interior is habitable and inhabited. Excluded by seismology: earthquake waves travel through a solid inner core.}

\gloss{Howe, Linda Moulton}{Investigative journalist and documentarian, best known for
\emph{A Strange Harvest} (1980) and \emph{Earthfiles}. See Chapter~\ref{ch:earthfiles}.}

\gloss{Hynek, J.\ Allen}{Astronomer, scientific consultant to Blue Book, later founder of the Center
for UFO Studies. The field's most consequential convert from sceptic to serious investigator.}

\gloss{Hypnagogia}{The transitional state between waking and sleep, which reliably produces vivid imagery and sound that is indistinguishable at the time from perception.}

\gloss{Hypnotic regression}{The use of hypnosis to attempt retrieval of forgotten experience. It does not recover memory; it generates narrative which the subject afterwards believes. Testimony whose only source is regression has no evidentiary base.}

\glossletter{I}

\gloss{IFO}{Identified Flying Object. What the overwhelming majority of UFOs become on investigation.}

\gloss{Illusory truth effect}{The experimentally robust finding that repeated statements are judged
more likely to be true, irrespective of source credibility. See Section~\ref{sec:powerpropaganda}.}

\gloss{Immaculate Constellation}{The codename of an alleged unacknowledged special access programme,
described in an eleven-page whistleblower report published in October 2024 and entered into the
Congressional Record on 13 November 2024. The Department of Defense states it has no record of any such
programme. See Section~\ref{sec:immaculate}.}

\gloss{Interdimensional hypothesis}{The proposal that the phenomenon originates in another dimension or plane rather than another star system. It explains everything and forbids nothing, which is its problem.}

\glossletter{J}

\gloss{JANET}{The unmarked airline that carries cleared employees from a private terminal at
Las Vegas Harry Reid International to Groom Lake, Tonopah and the Nevada National Security
Site. Operating since 1972; radio call sign ``Janet''. The expansion ``Joint Air Network for
Employee Transportation'' is popular but unconfirmed. See Section~\ref{sec:janet}.}

\glossletter{K}

\gloss{Kardashev scale}{A classification of hypothetical civilisations by the energy they command: planetary, stellar, galactic. A bookkeeping device, not a measurement.}

\gloss{Kean, Leslie}{Investigative journalist, author of \emph{UFOs: Generals, Pilots and Government
Officials Go on the Record} (2010) and co-author of the December 2017 \emph{New York Times} report
that disclosed AATIP.}

\gloss{Kecksburg}{The December 1965 recovery of an acorn-shaped object near Kecksburg, Pennsylvania, after a widely observed fireball. The military removed something; the record of what has never been produced.}

\gloss{Knapp, George}{Chief investigative reporter at KLAS-TV, Las Vegas. Broke the Bob Lazar story in
1989 and co-wrote \emph{Hunt for the Skinwalker}. See Sections~\ref{sec:s4lazar}
and~\ref{sec:skinwalker}.}

\glossletter{L}

\gloss{Leir, Roger}{Podiatric surgeon (1935--2014) who removed and analysed objects he described as
alien implants. The analyses were never independently replicated.}

\gloss{Lemuria}{A hypothetical submerged landmass proposed by Philip Sclater in 1864 to explain lemur
distribution, superseded by plate tectonics and subsequently absorbed into occult literature. See
Section~\ref{sec:ileslemuria}.}

\gloss{Lenticular cloud}{A stationary, smooth, lens-shaped cloud formed in the lee of terrain. One of
the most reliable producers of genuine ``flying saucer'' photographs.}

\gloss{LIDAR}{Light Detection and Ranging. An active laser system that measures range by timing
reflected pulses, producing precise three-dimensional point clouds of solid surfaces. It is a
surveying instrument: excellent for terrain and structure, largely blind to the low-reflectivity
luminous phenomena most often reported. See Section~\ref{sec:equipment}.}

\glossletter{M}

\gloss{Majestic 12}{A purported secret committee described in documents that surfaced in 1984. The
documents are generally regarded as fabricated, on typographic and procedural grounds.}

\gloss{Mandela effect}{A widely shared, confidently held false memory of a public fact. Evidence that consensus recollection can be wrong at scale, which is why agreement between witnesses is not by itself corroboration.}

\gloss{Men in Black}{Figures reported to appear after sightings to intimidate witnesses. See
Chapter~\ref{ch:government}.}

\gloss{METI}{Messaging Extraterrestrial Intelligence: deliberately transmitting rather than only listening. Distinct from SETI, and ethically contested for the reason Hawking gave --- a transmission cannot be recalled.}

\gloss{Missing 411}{David Paulides's series cataloguing disappearances in wilderness areas. Notable for what it declines to claim: it implies a cause without stating one, which is a rhetorical strategy rather than a hypothesis.}

\gloss{Missing time}{A reported unaccounted interval, typically minutes to hours, taken in abduction research as an indicator of an unremembered event. It is a report about the estimation of duration, which is one of the least reliable things humans do.}

\gloss{MJ-12}{See \textbf{Majestic 12}. The abbreviation used in the documents themselves and in most of the secondary literature.}

\gloss{MK-ULTRA}{The CIA's 1953--1973 programme of behavioural and drug experimentation on subjects who mostly did not consent. It matters here because it is documented: it establishes that the agency did run unethical experiments, which is why comparable claims cannot be dismissed on principle alone.}

\gloss{Montauk Project}{Alleged time-travel and mind-control experiments at Camp Hero, Long Island, sourced entirely to regressed testimony. See \textbf{Hypnotic regression}.}

\gloss{MUFON}{Mutual UFO Network. The largest civilian UFO reporting organisation, founded in 1969. It collects case reports on a standard form, which makes it a database of reports rather than of measurements.}

\gloss{Mutilation (cattle)}{Livestock deaths featuring apparently precise tissue removal and reported
absence of blood. See Chapter~\ref{ch:earthfiles}.}

\glossletter{N}

\gloss{Nazca Lines}{The geoglyphs on the Peruvian coastal plain, made roughly 500 BCE to 500 CE. Reproducible with rope, stakes and time, and demonstrated to be so.}

\gloss{New World Order}{A conspiracy framework alleging a covert global authority behind nominal
governments. Included here with the point the government chapter makes explicitly: the older versions
of this framework are antisemitic in origin, and that origin is not incidental to the versions still in
circulation.}

\gloss{Nibiru}{Sitchin's proposed planet on a 3,600-year orbit. No such body appears in solar-system dynamics or in any sky survey.}

\gloss{NICAP}{National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena. The civilian pressure group of 1956--1980 that pushed for congressional hearings, notable for recruiting retired officers to lend it institutional standing.}

\gloss{NIDS}{National Institute for Discovery Science. Robert Bigelow's privately funded research body (1995--2004), and the first organisation to investigate Skinwalker Ranch.}

\gloss{Nimitz encounter}{A series of November 2004 radar and visual contacts by aircraft from Carrier
Strike Group Eleven off Southern California, including the FLIR1 ``Tic Tac'' video. See
Section~\ref{sec:uap}.}

\gloss{Non-human intelligence (NHI)}{The current preferred term for the hypothesised authors of
UAP, replacing ``extraterrestrial'' because it leaves the origin --- other planet, other dimension,
terrestrial but non-human --- unspecified. That breadth is its attraction and its weakness: a claim
that commits to nothing cannot be falsified by anything. See Chapter~\ref{ch:language} and
Section~\ref{sec:ufocults}.}

\gloss{NUFORC}{National UFO Reporting Center. A hotline and public database of self-submitted sightings: useful for volume and pattern, and of no use for provenance.}

\gloss{Null hypothesis}{The default assumption that an observed effect is absent, or attributable to
chance. It is not a belief; it is a starting position that evidence must move.}

\glossletter{O}

\gloss{Occam's razor}{The principle that, among explanations accounting equally well for the evidence,
the one requiring the fewest unsupported assumptions is preferred. It is a heuristic for choosing
what to test first, not a proof.}

\gloss{Operation Paperclip}{The postwar U.S.\ programme that recruited German scientists and engineers,
with direct consequences for aerospace development and, indirectly, for the flying-saucer era.}

\gloss{`Oumuamua}{The first confirmed interstellar object, observed in October 2017, whose non-gravitational acceleration prompted Avi Loeb's artificial-origin proposal.}

\glossletter{P}

\gloss{Panspermia}{The hypothesis that life, or its chemical precursors, arrived on Earth from space. It relocates the origin problem rather than solving it.}

\gloss{Paperclip}{See Operation Paperclip.}

\gloss{Parallax}{The apparent change in an object's position caused by motion of the observer rather than of the object. Responsible for most apparent instantaneous accelerations on gimballed sensor video.}

\gloss{Parapsychology}{The scientific study of claimed anomalous mental phenomena. See
Section~\ref{sec:parapsych}.}

\gloss{Pareidolia}{The perception of a meaningful pattern --- usually a face or a craft --- in ambiguous visual noise. The default explanation for most anomalous imagery, and the one that must be excluded first.}

\gloss{Peer review}{Pre-publication evaluation of a manuscript by other specialists. It filters, it
does not certify. See Section~\ref{sec:deconstructingpeerreview}.}

\gloss{Phoenix Lights}{The mass sighting over Arizona on 13 March 1997, comprising two separate events: an aircraft formation, and later flares dropped over the Barry Goldwater Range.}

\gloss{Pole shift}{Ambiguous term. Distinguish geomagnetic reversal (real, slow), true polar wander
(real, very slow), and catastrophic crustal displacement (not supported). See Section~\ref{sec:sciencepoleshifting}.}

\gloss{Pope, Nick}{British civil servant (1965--2026) who ran the Ministry of Defence UFO desk from
1991 to 1994 and later became the subject's most widely quoted commentator. See
Section~\ref{sec:rendlesham}.}

\gloss{Precognition}{Claimed acquisition of information about future events by non-inferential means.}

\gloss{Project Mogul}{The classified high-altitude balloon programme for detecting Soviet nuclear tests, offered by the Air Force in 1994 as the explanation for the Roswell debris.}

\gloss{Project Sign / Project Grudge}{The first two U.S.\ Air Force UFO investigations --- Sign in 1948, Grudge from 1949 to 1951 --- preceding Blue Book. Sign took the subject seriously; Grudge was built to make it go away.}

\gloss{Provenance}{Where a document, image or sample actually came from, established independently of what it appears to show. In this book an image without provenance is not weak evidence; it is not evidence.}

\gloss{Psi}{Collective term for claimed anomalous phenomena studied by parapsychology, covering both
ESP and psychokinesis.}

\gloss{Psychokinesis (PK)}{Claimed direct mental influence on physical systems. Conventionally divided
into macro-PK (visible object movement) and micro-PK (statistical deviation in random systems).}

\glossletter{R}

\gloss{Radiosonde}{A balloon-borne instrument package. The Roswell debris was officially attributed to
equipment of this general class, specifically a Project Mogul array. See Chapter~\ref{ch:roswell}.}

\gloss{Remote viewing}{Claimed perception of distant locations, investigated under U.S.\ government
funding in programmes including Stargate.}

\gloss{REM-POD}{A consumer ghost-hunting device that sounds a tone when the electromagnetic field around its antenna is disturbed. It responds to any conductive mass, including the operator.}

\gloss{Rendlesham Forest}{The December 1980 incident outside RAF Woodbridge, Suffolk, involving USAF personnel, Colonel Halt's contemporaneous tape and memorandum, and Sgt Penniston's notebook. The best-documented military case in the record, and the book's standing example of a document being worth more than a conclusion.}

\gloss{Rio Scale}{A numerical scale for the significance and reliability of a claimed detection of extraterrestrial intelligence. The field's one serious attempt at calibrated language.}

\gloss{Robertson Panel}{The CIA-convened scientific panel of January 1953 which recommended stripping UFO reports of their special status and using mass media to reduce public interest. The clearest documented instance of deliberate official debunking.}

\gloss{Roswell}{The July 1947 debris recovery near Roswell, New Mexico. The founding case of American
Ufology. See Chapter~\ref{ch:roswell}.}

\glossletter{S}

\gloss{Scepticism}{The practice of proportioning belief to evidence, including belief in one's own
conclusions. Not the same as denial, which is a conclusion held in advance.}

\gloss{SETI}{Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence. Primarily radio and optical survey work; see
Sections~\ref{sec:seti} and~18.7 for the case that its search space is minute.}

\gloss{Shostak, Seth}{Senior astronomer at the SETI Institute and the most prominent public sceptic
of the visitation hypothesis.}

\gloss{Skinwalker Ranch}{A property in the Uintah Basin, Utah, under continuous privately funded investigation since 1996 --- NIDS, then AAWSAP, then television. The only site in the field with sustained instrumentation, and the standing example of measurement without controls.}

\gloss{Sleep paralysis}{A normal state in which the muscle atonia of REM sleep persists into waking, frequently accompanied by a felt presence, pressure on the chest and inability to move. It explains the form of many bedroom encounters without impugning anyone's sincerity.}

\gloss{Sonoluminescence}{Light emission from collapsing cavitation bubbles --- included here as a
reminder that genuinely bizarre physical phenomena exist and were established by measurement.}

\gloss{Spectrometer}{An instrument that disperses light by wavelength, allowing emission or absorption
features to be matched against known materials. The single most diagnostic tool available to a field
investigator, because it can distinguish a sodium street lamp, a magnesium flare and a plasma
discharge from one another. See Section~\ref{sec:equipment}.}

\gloss{STARGATE (Project)}{The umbrella name for the U.S.\ remote-viewing programme run at Fort Meade and SRI until 1995, closed after an external review found no operational intelligence value.}

\gloss{Strawman (straw man)}{A misrepresentation of an opponent's position, built so that it can
be defeated more easily than the real one, after which the real one is treated as defeated too. It is
the most common failure mode on both sides of this subject, which is why it is named here rather than
left implied. The sceptic's version: ``believers think every light in the sky is a spacecraft,'' when
the serious claim is that a residual fraction of well-documented cases is unexplained. The
believer's version: ``science refuses to look,'' when the actual position is that the evidence
offered so far has not met the standard --- a statement about evidence, not about willingness. The ``swamp gas''
episode of Chapter~\ref{ch:government} is a worked example: a real mechanism was applied to cases it
could not account for, and demolishing that explanation was then mistaken for demolishing the
sightings themselves. The repair is procedural and cheap. State the other
position in the form its holder would accept, get that agreed, and only then argue with it ---
which is the same discipline the whole of Chapter~\ref{ch:language} is about.}

\gloss{Swamp gas}{Methane from decaying organic matter, capable of producing ignis fatuus. Notorious
as an over-applied official explanation, which damaged the credibility of subsequent correct ones.}

\glossletter{T}

\gloss{Taylor, Travis S.}{Aerospace and optical engineer, chief scientist at Radiance Technologies
and on-camera investigator at Skinwalker Ranch. See Section~\ref{sec:ufologist}.}

\gloss{Telepathy}{Claimed direct mind-to-mind transfer of information.}

\gloss{Temperature inversion}{An atmospheric layer in which temperature rises with altitude, capable
of refracting light and radar and producing displaced or spurious returns. One of the most important
mundane explanations in the field.}

\gloss{Tic Tac}{Informal name for the object in the 2004 \emph{Nimitz} FLIR1 footage.}

\gloss{Trace evidence}{Physical residue associated with a reported event --- soil alteration, radiation,
material fragments. The category the field most needs and least has.}

\glossletter{U}

\gloss{UAP}{Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (formerly Unidentified Aerial Phenomena). The current
official term, broadened in 2022 to include submerged and transmedium objects.}

\gloss{UFO}{Unidentified Flying Object. Coined by the U.S.\ Air Force in 1953 to replace ``flying
saucer''. Note what it does and does not assert: it is a statement about the observer's knowledge, not
about the object.}

\gloss{UFODAP}{UFO Data Acquisition Project. A multi-sensor automated monitoring station combining
optical and thermal cameras with magnetometer, radiation and RF sensors under common triggering and
time-stamping. Its value lies in recording several independent channels simultaneously; its limitation
is that it must be sited in advance, where nothing may happen. See Section~\ref{sec:equipment}.}

\gloss{Ufology}{The scientific study of UFOs and the fringe: the disciplined investigation of
unidentified aerial, submerged and transmedium phenomena together with the wider body of anomalous
claims that surrounds them --- abductions, cryptids, lost civilisations, conspiracy material and the
rest. The word carries no commitment to any explanation. What makes the work scientific is not the
subject matter but the method: reconstruct the observing conditions first, name the sources, publish
the null results, and let the residual be as small as the evidence requires.}

\gloss{USAP}{Unacknowledged Special Access Program. A classified programme whose existence, not merely
whose contents, is itself classified --- so that officials are obliged to deny it. This creates an
epistemic trap: denial is exactly what one would expect whether the programme exists or not. See
Section~\ref{sec:immaculate}.}

\gloss{USO}{Unidentified Submerged Object. See Section~\ref{sec:usos}.}

\glossletter{V}

\gloss{Varginha}{The January 1996 events in Varginha, Brazil, in which witnesses reported a captured creature and a soldier later died. The most heavily investigated non-U.S.\ case, and the one where official denial and witness testimony stand furthest apart.}

\gloss{Vimana}{Flying vehicles or palaces described in Sanskrit literature: read by ancient-astronaut authors as engineering documents, and by Sanskritists as mythological literature.}

\glossletter{W}

\gloss{Walton, Travis}{The logger at the centre of the November 1975 Arizona case, in which six co-workers reported him taken and subsequently passed polygraph examinations. Filmed in 1993 as \emph{Fire in the Sky}, a title which has largely replaced the case in public memory.}

\gloss{Wormhole}{A hypothetical topological connection between separated regions of spacetime. Permitted
by general relativity as a solution; not demonstrated to exist, and not demonstrated to be traversable.
See Chapter~\ref{ch:dimensions}.}
