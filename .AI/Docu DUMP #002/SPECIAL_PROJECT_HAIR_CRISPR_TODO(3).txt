- Must verify: knockout does NOT cascade into lymphatic, cardiovascular, or other critical systems

[ ] 1.4 — Cross-reference ALL candidates against reptile anti-node map
- Anti-node genes: Lef1, Sox2, Sox18, Foxi3
- These are the "total baldness switch" — knocking them out deletes ALL hair types at once
- QUESTION: Do we want the total switch or the differentiation genes?
- Total switch = faster, all at once, but no serial mapping possible
- Differentiation genes = serial mapping possible, one hair type at a time
- RECOMMENDATION: Start with differentiation genes for the causal map, then validate
  anti-node genes as the MASTER OFF SWITCH as confirmation

- Determine: are these SUBTYPES of the 4 main programs, or DISTINCT programs requiring

PHASE 2: DELIVERY MECHANISM RESEARCH
==========================================================

[ ] 2.1 — Topical CRISPR delivery via lipid nanoparticles (LNPs)
- LNP formulations that penetrate follicular ostia (the follicle opening)
- Studies: LNP-mRNA delivery to skin (Moderna/Pfizer COVID tech used LNPs — same platform)
- Advantage: surface application, no needles
- Question: can LNPs reach follicle STEM CELLS (bulge region), not just the shaft?

[ ] 2.2 — Intradermal microinjection
- Direct injection into dermal papilla of target follicles
- Feasibility: dermatologists already do this (fillers, steroids for alopecia)
- Question: injection depth required to hit bulge stem cells (~1.5-2mm deep)
- Question: how many injection sites per body region?

[ ] 2.3 — Electroporation-assisted delivery
- Use electrical pulses to open cell membranes for CRISPR uptake after topical/injected delivery
- Devices: existing electroporation tools used in DNA vaccine delivery
- Question: skin tissue tolerance — pain, scarring risk

[ ] 2.4 — Viral vectors (AAV) — LIKELY UNSUITABLE but document why
- AAV = gold standard for in-vivo gene therapy
- Problem: permanent integration, off-target risk, immune response
- Problem: AAV spreads beyond application site — violates head hair guardrail
  (NOTE: head hair now included in knockout, but spread to non-target tissue still a problem)
- Document: why viral vectors are ETHICALLY and PRACTICALLY wrong for SPH

[ ] 2.5 — Ex vivo approach: extract follicle stem cells, edit, re-implant
- Remove body follicle stem cells via biopsy
- CRISPR edit in lab with full off-target screening
- Re-implant edited cells
- Advantage: zero off-target risk in vivo
- Disadvantage: extremely labor-intensive, not scalable
- Assess: could this work for SMALL areas (face, neck) as proof of concept?

[ ] 2.6 — Ribonucleoprotein (RNP) delivery — CRISPR as protein+guide, no DNA
- CRISPR Cas9 protein + guide RNA delivered as pre-formed complex
- Advantage: NO foreign DNA integrates, editing is transient, degrades after action
- Advantage: faster editing, lower off-target than plasmid/viral
- This is the LEADING candidate for somatic cosmetic gene editing
- Deep dive required on RNP + LNP or RNP + cell-penetrating peptide delivery
