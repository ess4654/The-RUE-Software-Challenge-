  wilderness, the exile, the counterfeit — these are HEBREW themes
  that Revelation wraps in Greek apocalyptic language. The series
  is not a conversion tool. It is a pattern atlas. Shapiro can
  endorse the ATLAS without endorsing the CHRISTOLOGY.

  Peterson has described himself as "very religious" but stops

  BOTH Knowles (too secular, strips the supernatural) AND Shapiro
  (too Christian-adjacent, still implies Christ as archetype).
  He's the bridge. Bridges get hit from both sides.
